## 3. Unverified or assumed

- Performance targets, the 150 ms mock latency and the shared-password model are my defaults.
- "Recorded fixtures" come from my own mock, so they are synthetic.
- Opening the deck in PowerPoint is unconfirmed.
- The JQL `updated` timestamp is built in UTC.
- The Epic due date is taken as the end of that day in the report time zone.
- Only the winning RAG level's reasons are listed.
- The overall percent in the trend is the mean of real Epics' percent.
